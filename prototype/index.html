      Prototype 0.20 · The Grand Kitchen

      Tiny TitansFood Fight

      You're frog-sized, and the whole kitchen is the arena: a 120 m island, a 440 m back counter with the sink and stove, a dining table to hide under and a floor the size of a town. Food is the only weapon. Play in first person against 11 bots until the Soap Tide closes in, or online against your friends. Blueberries fire like a machine gun.

      Bots

      Easy bots aim loosely, react slowly and rarely dodge. Hard bots lead their shots, throw faster and dodge often.

      Choose a mode

      Pick 3 foods. Bots bring 3 random foods each.

      Start with a utensil (optional). Bots may bring one too.

      Everyone who opens this game and types the same room code plays together. To invite friends, share this page with them from the Share menu; they need to be signed in to Claude.

      On the menu today

        Support utensils

        Utensils arrive in delivery boxes that parachute into the kitchen (follow the coloured light beams). Walk into a box to open it and carry the utensil, or splat it open with food from range. You carry up to three and switch between them like food; with all three full, a new one swaps out the one in your hand, and knocked-out Titans drop all of theirs. A utensil boosts every food you throw, even the banana peel trap for some, but the food is still the shot, and every boost has a cost.

          Hazards: the three burners cycle on (don't stand on them), the sink and spilled water make you Wet (cleanses sticky and fire, but freezes last longer), honey pools refill your Glaze shield, and the red spatula pads on the floor fling you up onto the counters and the table. Smash the giant tomato for extra ammo. The floor is scattered with giant fallen food and knocked-over chairs to hide behind and climb, a pickle jar you can walk into, and a cracked egg whose white is slippery.

          Prototype of the Tiny Titans design document: 12 of 30 foods, the whole kitchen, 12 Titans, bots only.
