    Prototype 0.2 · The Grand Kitchen

    Tiny TitansFood Fight

    You're frog-sized, and the whole kitchen is the arena: a 120 m island, a 440 m back counter with the sink and stove, a dining table to hide under and a floor the size of a town. Food is the only weapon. Drop in with 11 bots, 200 health and a double jump, and be the last Titan standing when the Soap Tide closes in.
