      Prototype 0.12 · The Grand Kitchen

      Tiny TitansFood Fight

      You're frog-sized, and the whole kitchen is the arena: a 120 m island, a 440 m back counter with the sink and stove, a dining table to hide under and a floor the size of a town. Food is the only weapon. Play in first person against 11 bots until the Soap Tide closes in, or online against your friends. Blueberries fire like a machine gun.

      Choose a mode

      Pick 3 foods. Bots bring 3 random foods each.

      Everyone who opens this game and types the same room code plays together. To invite friends, share this page with them from the Share menu; they need to be signed in to Claude.
