# Ultimate Alt Food Ability Update

Ideas for a new update that gives each food an alternate ability, typed up
from handwritten notes. Spelling is cleaned up, but the ideas are exactly as
written.

1. **Tomato – Dash.** Uses half the stamina a normal dash uses. If the player
   runs into another player or a wall during the dash, it makes a big,
   damaging splat that uses up another tomato.

2. **Ice Cube – Storm.** Throws an ice ball made of several ice cubes. Where it
   lands, a whirlwind appears that pulls every player toward its center
   (like the Tornado spell in Clash Royale) and slows them.

3. **Watermelon – Smash.** While holding a watermelon in mid-air, the player
   smashes down. When they hit the floor it deals damage in an area around
   them. The higher the player smashes from, the bigger the damage and the
   area.

4. **Soda Can – Jump.** If the player is on the floor, they do a mega jump that
   damages every enemy close by.

5. **Grape – Juice.** A giant ball of juice that can be thrown. It splats on the
   floor and slows down everyone who touches it.

6. **Pineapple – Spikes.** Summons a giant line of spikes that pop up one by
   one. A player hit by a spike just as it appears gets knocked back.

7. **Jello – Jump Pads.** Jump pads that boost players higher and have a
   durability meter.

8. **Carrot – Teleport.** Uses 3 carrots to throw a teleport carrot that
   teleports the player to where it lands. It has a long charge-up, and if
   the player gets hit during it, the ability stops. Teleport carrots also
   fly slower than normal carrots.

9. **Blueberries – Spiral.** Shoots a spiraling ring of blueberries that fly out
   in every direction around the player for 2 seconds.

10. **Chili Pepper – Flamethrower.** The flames stay on the ground for a while
    and damage everyone who touches them. The flamethrower is very close
    range.
